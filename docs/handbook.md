# Employee Handbook: Aura Coffee House

## 1. Introduction and Mission
Welcome to Aura Coffee House. Our mission is to provide a sanctuary for our community through high-quality craft coffee, artisanal pastries, and exceptional service. We believe that every cup of coffee is 
an opportunity to create a connection. Our goal is to be the neighborhood's "third place"—a home away from home where everyone feels welcome.

## 2. Staff Roles and Responsibilities
**Baristas:** The primary role of the Barista is to craft high-quality beverages. They are responsible for operating the espresso machines, maintaining the "dial-in" of the grinders, creating latte art, and 
ensuring the beverage station remains clean.

**Servers:** Servers focus on the dining area and food service. They are responsible for greeting guests, taking food orders, clearing tables, and ensuring the seating area is tidy and inviting.

**Shift Supervisors:** Supervisors oversee the flow of the floor during a shift. They handle cash drawer reconciliation, manage staff breaks, resolve customer complaints, and ensure all opening/closing 
checklists are completed.

## 3. Opening Procedures
The shop opens at 7:00 AM daily. The opening team must arrive by 6:15 AM.
1. **Power Up:** Turn on the espresso machines, grinders, and ovens.
2. **Calibration:** Dial in the espresso shots to ensure the correct yield and extraction time.
3. **Pastry Case:** Arrange fresh pastries in the display case and label them with prices.
4. **Station Prep:** Brew the first batches of drip coffee and prepare milk pitchers.
5. **Cleaning:** Sweep the entrance and wipe down all guest tables.

## 4. Closing Procedures
The shop closes at 9:00 PM daily. The closing team must complete the following by 10:00 PM.
1. **Equipment Shutdown:** Turn off the espresso machines and ovens. Backflush the group heads with cleaning powder.
2. **Cleaning:** Scrub the steam wands, wipe down the counters, and wash all remaining dishes.
3. **Waste Management:** Empty all trash cans and recycling bins.
4. **Financials:** Count the register drawer and submit the daily sales report to the manager.
5. **Security:** Ensure all windows are locked, the alarm is set, and the lights are off.

## 5. Customer Service Standards
We follow the "Three Smiles" rule: Smile when a customer enters, smile when taking their order, and smile when handing them their drink. 
*   **Greeting:** Always acknowledge a customer within 10 seconds of them walking through the door.
*   **Upselling:** If a customer orders a coffee, ask if they would like to try one of our warm butter croissants.
*   **Complaints:** If a customer is unhappy with a drink, apologize immediately and offer to remake it for free. If the issue persists, involve a Shift Supervisor.

## 6. The Menu Highlights
**House Blend:** A medium roast with notes of caramel and toasted hazelnut.
**Signature Latte:** A blend of our House Blend espresso, steamed oat milk, and a hint of Madagascar vanilla.
**Vegan Options:** We offer all specialty lattes with oat, almond, or soy milk. All pastries are labeled with "V" for vegan or "GF" for gluten-free.
**Seasonal Specials:** Our menu changes every three months. Please check the "Seasonal Specials" chalkboard near the register for current items.

## 7. Health, Safety, and Hygiene
*   **Hand Washing:** Staff must wash hands before handling food, after using the restroom, and every 60 minutes during a shift.
*   **Chemical Safety:** Only use approved cleaning agents on food-contact surfaces. Never mix bleach with ammonia.
*   **Spill Protocol:** If a spill occurs, "Stay and Stay": Stay with the spill to warn others, then immediately mark it with a "Wet Floor" sign and clean it up.
*   **Temperature Control:** Ensure the refrigerator stays between 34°F and 40°F. Check and log the temperature twice daily.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q: Do you offer Wi-Fi?**
A: Yes, our guest Wi-Fi is free. The password is printed on the bottom of the receipts.

**Q: Can I bring my own reusable mug?**
A: Yes! We offer a $0.50 discount for customers who bring their own reusable coffee cups.

**Q: Do you have a loyalty program?**
A: Yes, we offer a "Buy 9, Get the 10th Free" punch card, available for both coffee and tea.

**Q: Is there a seating limit?**
A: During peak hours (8:00 AM – 10:00 AM), we may request that customers limit their stay to one hour to accommodate our high volume of commuters.
